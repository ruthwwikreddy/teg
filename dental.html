Build a Successful Career in Dentistry with BDS Abroad

A Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) is the foundation of a rewarding and respected career in dentistry. This professional degree focuses on improving oral health and making a meaningful impact on people’s lives.

Studying BDS abroad offers students global exposure, advanced learning facilities, and excellent career opportunities with competitive salary packages.

Duration

5 Years

Average Tuition

AUD 387,280

Eligibility & Admission

  • Educational Qualification: Grade 12 from a recognized board with a science background (PCB).
  • NEET Qualification: Valid NEET scores for admission, especially for practicing in India later.
  • English Proficiency: Proof through tests like IELTS (typically 7.0 band).
