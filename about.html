Message From Our Director

Dr. Pritpal Singh Bagga, BDS, MPH (UK)

Having studied in the UK and working across different cultures, I have been fascinated by the world of opportunities that lie on foreign shores for higher education. The systems are flexible, the courses are par excellence and the tuition fees are very affordable. Added to that are the numerous internship opportunities that provide students with cutting-edge hands-on training, making them super suitable for a great future ahead.

In the due course of my work life in this industry, I have served the University of Buckingham, the UK as their Regional Manager and also The University of Huddersfield, the UK as their South Asia Representative and thus understand the intricacies of the overseas education industry from the deepest roots. My teams, share my experience vision, goal and commitment and we assure nothing but the best.

Our teams of IELTS certified trainers across several branches, working under my guidance make sure that our students are well equipped and clear the needed language examinations with cutting edge competence.

Understanding the importance of guided mentoring, I have dedicated myself and my teams in this field to ensure that the Indian students get the right direction and pursue the right course for a dream future ahead.

I am committed to doling out my years of knowledge in the industry and have mentored my teams to give in their best, so that students aspiring to study abroad, get placed in the right institutions according to their aptitude and budget.

"After desperately searching for colleges in India and realizing that all my efforts are in vain, I decided to pursue education abroad. My parents came across Dr. Pritpal and he truly saved me from all the hassles and scams of the Indian colleges which we know are prevalent in India. He told us about Debrecen Medical University in Hungary, which is one of the most reputed universities in central europe and guided us right from the start about what the course entails and everything we must know about the University and the city of Debrecen. I am now a 2nd year Medical student here and am grateful I had Dr Pritpal's guidance, he know's what he is doing and my parents and I highly recommend him"

- Aakash Sharma and family
