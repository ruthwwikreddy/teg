Study MBBS Abroad: Eligibility, Fees, Top Countries & Admission Process

Studying MBBS abroad has become a popular choice for Indian students who aim to build a successful career in medicine. With limited medical seats and high competition in India, many students prefer to pursue MBBS in foreign countries that offer quality education at affordable costs.

Countries such as Russia, Bangladesh, and Caribbean nations are among the top destinations for MBBS abroad. These countries provide globally recognized medical degrees, modern infrastructure, and English-medium programs, making them ideal for Indian students.

Eligibility

To study MBBS abroad, Indian students must meet the eligibility criteria set by the NMC:

  • Academic: Min 50% in PCB (12th standard)
  • Age: Min 17 years by Dec 31st
  • NEET: Must qualify NEET-UG

Documents

  • 10th and 12th marksheets
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Official admission letter
  • Valid student visa
  • Birth certificate & NEET score
  • Medical fitness certificate
